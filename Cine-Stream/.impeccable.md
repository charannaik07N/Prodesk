## Design Context

### Users

- Primary audience: students.
- Context: quick browsing sessions between classes, in transit, or during downtime.
- Core job to be done: find something to watch quickly with minimal decision friction.

### Brand Personality

- 3-word direction: cinematic, editorial, premium.
- Emotional tone: dark, dramatic, intense without being noisy.
- Interface should feel curated and confident rather than playful or overly technical.

### Aesthetic Direction

- Theme target: both light and dark, with dark as the hero experience.
- Visual language: poster-forward, typography-led, high-contrast editorial composition.
- Anti-reference: avoid blur-heavy glassmorphism UI patterns.

### Design Principles

- Speed to decision: prioritize scanning and immediate relevance over deep navigation.
- Poster first: artwork and title clarity should lead every layout choice.
- Editorial hierarchy: strong type scale and spacing rhythm, minimal chrome.
- Purposeful motion: subtle transitions that clarify state, not decorative over-animation.
- Consistent contrast: maintain strong readability in both dark and light themes.
